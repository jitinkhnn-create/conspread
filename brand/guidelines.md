## Non-negotiables
1. Every quotation is verbatim and traceable to a chapter file. No invented quotes. No fake chapter references.
2. Paraphrase must preserve the book's meaning.
3. No claim about outcomes, research or statistics unless it appears in the book. Even then, cite the chapter in `chapter_references`.
4. All content is safe for readers aged 11 to 15, including imagery.
5. No image ever carries text produced by an image model. All text is rendered deterministically.
6. Nothing auto-publishes. A human approves every post.

## Composition rules (structural, not stylistic)
- Canvas: carousel 1080×1350, single post 1080×1080.
- Safe margin: keep all text at least `design_system.layout.safe_margin_px` from each edge (Instagram crops the grid preview to 3:4 and 1:1).
- Slide 1 (hook) must be readable at thumbnail size: one idea, large type, no small print.
- One idea per slide. A carousel has one clear arc: hook → build → payoff → (optional) action.
- Emphasis: one emphasised phrase per slide at most, using the chosen `emphasis_style`.
- Text contrast: WCAG AA at minimum against its background (checked in visual QA).
- No slide is a wall of text. Word limits per track are in `brand/voice.md`.

## Imagery rules
- Allowed: abstract shapes, textures, simple illustrations, and photographic scenes with no identifiable real minors.
- Disallowed: stock-photo clichés of "sad teen alone", lightbulbs and brains, anything that stigmatises an emotion, any real-looking minor's face that could be mistaken for a real, specific child.
- Illustrations must feel age-neutral: not babyish (under 11) and not corporate.

## Logo / watermark
_Pending the cover and brand identity._ Placeholder rule: a small, consistent mark on the final slide only (carousel) or in one fixed corner (single post), never overlapping text.

## Review checklist (critic and human)

- [ ] Does it sound like `brand/voice.md`?
- [ ] Palette and type match `design_system.json`?
- [ ] Legible at thumbnail size?
- [ ] Nothing here could shame, alarm or mislead its audience?
