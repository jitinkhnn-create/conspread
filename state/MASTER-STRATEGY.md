# MASTER STRATEGY: Building Tomorrow on Instagram

> Owner: Routine 03 (weekly strategy) updates this file where the evidence supports a change.
> A human may edit it at any time. Until the first weekly run, everything below is the starting hypothesis.
> Status: **seed, no performance data yet.**

## Purpose
Instagram is the discovery layer for the book. Every post must be worth saving on its own. The book comes second.

## Cadence
2 to 3 posts per week. Publishing days are owned by Postiz, not by this pipeline.

## Audience tracks
| Track | Who | What they want | Share of posts (starting guess) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `child_teen` | Ages 11 to 15 | Something that feels true about their own life, quick to read, not preachy | ~60% |
| `parent_teacher` | Parents, teachers | A concrete thing to say or try with a young person | ~40% |

## Content mix (starting hypothesis)
- Frameworks and reframes: the strongest "save" candidates
- Questions and exercises: reflective, comment-friendly
- Parent and teacher tips: shareable
- Verbatim workbook quotes: sparingly, always grounded

## CTA policy
Most posts use `none`, `save`, `share` or `reflect`. `discover_book` appears at most about 1 in 5 posts, and never on the first post of a week.
