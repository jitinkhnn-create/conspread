Before deciding the quiet new kid is unfriendly, ask this.

Sometimes we make quick judgments about people before we know them. Someone is quiet on the first day, and a whole story gets built around it.

A short conversation to try with your child or class:

“Imagine you’re meeting a new student in your class. What if you decided they weren’t friendly just because they were quiet on the first day? How might that stop you from making a new friend?”

Then, once they have thought about it:

“So next time, let’s try to learn more about people before deciding what they’re like. Everyone has a story, just like you.”

Send this to a parent or teacher who could use it this week.
